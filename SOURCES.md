# Research Sources and Verdicts

Research was performed read-only as text. URLs are references, not runtime dependencies.

## League Profiles

The public repository includes no private league history. Users supply their own inert JSON profile locally when they want manager tendencies, keeper eligibility, or league-specific draft calibration.

## Comparable Benchmark

- [FantasyPros draft accuracy methodology](https://www.fantasypros.com/about/faq/football-draft-accuracy-methodology/): final preseason snapshots, half-PPR evaluation, defined player pools, and a lower-is-better Accuracy Gap.
- [2025 draft accuracy table](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/accuracy/draft.php?sort=WR): 212 qualifying experts.
- [2023–2025 multi-year accuracy](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/accuracy/multi-year-draft.php): rolling three-year placements.
- [2025 accuracy analysis](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/2025s-most-accurate-fantasy-football-draft-rankings/): Seth Miller first in 2025; Jody Smith first in the three-year window.
- [FantasyData 2025 half-PPR leaders](https://fantasydata.com/nfl/leaders/player/fantasy-points-half-ppr): final points used only after the historical black-box order is locked.
- [ESPN 2025 preseason Top 200](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/45042111/fantasy-football-rankings-2025-ppr): dated independent expert anchor for the historical pilot cohort.
- [Mike Clay 2025 NFL Projection Guide](https://g.espncdn.com/s/ffldraftkit/25/NFLDK2025_CS_ClayProjections2025.pdf): September 4 preseason points, receptions, category leaders, projected schedule strength and unit grades. The historical lab converts its PPR point projections to half PPR and derives replacement baselines from QB12, RB30, WR36 and TE12.
- Archived ESPN and Mike Clay preseason guides remain recommended sources for users who expand the public one-season validation pilot. Any added cohort should preserve a dated pre-Week 1 lock and keep final outcomes isolated until evaluation.

**Verdict:** This is the only broadly comparable public expert dataset located. Compare experts within the same season and methodology. Do not equate the retired 2010–2014 PAY% method, current Accuracy Gap, draft accuracy, and weekly accuracy.

## Requested Sources

### Pat Fitzmaurice

FantasyPros lists Fitzmaurice 47th of 212 for 2025 draft accuracy and 36th for 2023–2025, with a stronger three-year TE placement of 14th. Verdict: moderate-positive evidence, especially at TE, but not sufficient for single-source rankings.

### Yahoo

The 2025 FantasyPros table lists Scott Pianowski 108th, Justin Boone 114th, and Matt Harmon 176th. Individual positional results vary. Verdict: mixed named-analyst evidence; no Yahoo brand-level accuracy bonus.

### ESPN

ESPN preserves strong public ranking archives, including a final [2025 PPR ranking hub](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/44786976/fantasy-football-rankings-2025-draft-ppr). The public PPR list cannot be assumed to equal a FantasyPros half-PPR contest submission. Verdict: useful independent projection voice, but current comparable accuracy was not verified.

### The Fantasy Footballers

The [Footballers accuracy page](https://www.thefantasyfootballers.com/accuracy/) lists selected historical FantasyPros placements and says recent submissions stopped. Verdict: useful archival context, not a complete independently reproducible accuracy series.

### Sleeper

Sleeper publishes tiers, mock drafts, ADP, and retrospectives, such as its [2024 RB tiers](https://sleeper.com/blog/fantasy-football-rb-tier-based-rankings-2024/). Verdict: platform/market signal, not one analyst with a public accuracy score.

Matt Harmon remains valuable as a receiver specialist even though his 2023-2025 overall draft-accuracy placement was 117th. His public rankings and Reception Perception methodology are therefore shown as a named dissent signal, not silently blended as if they were a proven superior overall board. Reception Perception's full 2026 tiers and sortable charting are subscriber products; the app does not bypass access controls or fabricate unavailable route metrics.

## Known Gaps

- Exact raw preseason contest submissions are generally not preserved as a complete downloadable public dataset.
- Public rankings may be overwritten in place or differ from contest scoring formats.
- Narrative MVP/bust articles are not full-list accuracy evaluations.
- Sleeper ADP and platform projections should not be confused with similarly named independent analysts.

## August 14, 2026 Analytics Research Pass

- [PFF Actual Opportunity](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-actual-opportunity-an-introduction-for-2018) values carries and targets by field position, target depth, down, and distance. It supports replacing raw-volume grades with expected fantasy opportunity when reproducible inputs are available.
- [PFF Weighted Opportunity](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-weighted-opportunity-a-better-fantasy-football-predictor-than-raw-touches) reports that targets are materially more valuable than carries and that location-aware opportunity tracks fantasy production better than raw touches.
- [Reception Perception validation](https://receptionperception.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/RPsummary_Success_Alignment_Outcomes_Final.pdf) supports routes plus coverage-specific success as a WR signal, while also showing that alignment changes how zone success should be interpreted.
- [ESPN Receiver Tracking Metrics](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/34649390/espn-receiver-tracking-metrics-how-new-nfl-stats-work-open-catch-yac-scores) evaluates getting open, catching, and YAC on every route, including untargeted routes. Use it as a player-skill split rather than a workload substitute.
- [nflfastR expected pass rate](https://nflfastr.com/reference/add_xpass.html) supplies game-state-adjusted pass probability and pass rate over expectation. PROE is preferable to raw pass rate for team tendency context.
- [ESPN positional schedule validation](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/11348957/fantasy-football-positional-strength-schedule-projections-little-merit) found substantial year-to-year movement in fantasy points allowed. Season-long schedule weight was reduced to a tiebreaker.
- [PLOS One NFL market study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0287601) found that point spreads and totals captured most median-outcome variability in its NFL sample. Frozen implied team totals are a promising touchdown-environment input, but remain sandboxed to prevent projection duplication.
- [PlayerProfiler Breakout Finder](https://www.playerprofiler.com/article/2022-breakout-finder-guide/) documents a rookie model using draft capital, draft age, early declaration, size, athleticism, breakout age, and college production. The app records these as candidates rather than treating the model as independently validated.
- [ESPN run-block win rate](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/29813062/introducing-new-nfl-run-blocking-run-stopping-stats-how-run-block-win-rate-run-stop-win-rate-work) and [pass-block win rate](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/24892208/creating-better-nfl-pass-blocking-pass-rushing-stats-analytics-explainer-faq-how-work) provide tracking-based line context designed to separate blocking from runner and quarterback results.

**Sandbox result:** Across 210 frozen 2021–2025 player-seasons, a fixed 35% projection-rank blend improved within-position MAE by only 0.029 ranks (4.886 to 4.857). It won two seasons, lost two, and tied one, so it was not promoted. Availability and disagreement mean penalties were also rejected. `research-lab.js` reproduces the experiment locally.
