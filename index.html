    How to read every rankings column
    Model

    Overall order from the weighted model. Lower rank is better.

    Comparison

    The selected platform, market, or analyst rank. Model edge shows value when this model ranks a player earlier and reach when it ranks him later.

    Player

    Current team, position rank, rookie status, and draft status. Click the name for the full profile.

    Tier

    Players with similar draft value. A tier break matters more than a one-spot rank difference.

    Base

    The dated ESPN/FantasyPros baseline rank before this league's model adjustments. Lower is better.

    Proj

    Mike Clay projected 2026 half-PPR season points. Higher is better; missing values remain blank.

    VOR

    Projection value over the flex-optimized replacement player, normalized 0–100. Higher is better.

    Opp

    Projected opportunity from attempts, carries, targets, and team shares, normalized 0–100. Higher means more volume.

    Schedule

    Composite ease score, normalized 0–100. Higher is easier. REG is Weeks 1–14; PO is Weeks 15–17.

    Durability

    Availability score using history, age curve, workload, and projected games. Higher is safer; confidence reflects sample size.

    Environment

    OFF is offense quality, OL is offensive line, and CAST is position-specific supporting cast. Higher is better.

    Market

    ECR minus ADP. Positive means experts rank the player earlier than the market, suggesting potential value.
